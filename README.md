# AI Career Stress Test

Test how exposed any job is to AI and robots, task by task, and get a plan to stay relevant.

Take the test: https://ai-career-stress-test.github.io/

- **Use any AI:** copy a ready-made prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, Muse or any assistant, then paste the answer back to score and compare AIs.
- **Score it yourself:** answer four quick questions about each task. No account needed.
- Installable on phones and computers, and works offline after the first visit.

## Venture Compass

Free business advisor: https://ai-career-stress-test.github.io/venture-compass/

- **Quick check or deep review:** describe the business, or add recent activities, rough numbers and files.
- **Any AI with web search** checks current news and industry data, then the page shows a compass reading, ranked next steps, trends, risks, Google Alerts to set, resources and sources.
- **Private by design:** no server or database. Files are attached in the person's own AI, never sent to this site.

## University editions

Each school gets its own version of the Stress Test that hands students off to that school's career center and tools.

- Open one with `?school=<id>`, for example https://ai-career-stress-test.github.io/?school=yu
- Schools are set up in `schools.js`: copy the `yu` block, change the names, wording and links, and give it a new id.
- `status: "proposal"` shows a notice that the school hasn't approved it yet. Change it to `"partner"` only after the school agrees in writing.
- Every result can be printed or downloaded as a one-page advisor summary.
