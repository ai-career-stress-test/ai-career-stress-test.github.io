How the score works

  1. Split the job into tasks. Jobs change one task at a time.
  2. Weight each task by its share of a working week.
  3. Score exposure 0–100: how much of the task AI software, agents or robots can do at equal or better quality and cost.
  4. Average by time. The overall score is the time-weighted average of the task scores.
Automate
AI or robots can do most of it.
Augment
AI does part. You direct and check.
Human-led
Your judgment, hands or trust carry it.

Bands: under 30 low exposure for now · 30–59 partly exposed · 60+ largely exposed. Self-scoring weights: how repeatable 35, where it is done 30, consequence of error 20, need for trust 15. A task scoring 65+ counts as Automate, 35–64 as Augment, under 35 as Human-led. All scores are informed estimates, not predictions. Confirm any certification with the issuing body before enrolling.
