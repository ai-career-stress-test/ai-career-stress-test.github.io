Before you attach any files

Your files go only to the AI you choose, never to Venture Compass. That AI's own privacy rules apply, and some AI services use chats to improve their models unless you switch that off in their settings.

  • Remove or black out passwords, bank and card numbers, and tax ID numbers.
  • Remove the names, phone numbers and emails of customers and staff.
  • Don't share anything under a confidentiality agreement (NDA), or trade secrets such as recipes, formulas or source code.
  • Where you can, share summaries or ranges instead of full statements.

How Venture Compass works

  1. Pick a depth. A quick check needs a few lines about the business. A deep review adds your recent activities, rough numbers and files, so the advice can be more specific.
  2. Your AI does the research. With web search on, it checks current news, prices, rules and competitors for your industry and location, then lists its sources.
  3. Read your report. A compass reading across five areas, ranked next steps, the trends that matter, risks, and resources.
  4. Keep watching. Set the suggested free Google Alerts so news comes to you, and run Venture Compass again each month.

Privacy and limits

  • Venture Compass has no server and no database. What you type, your files and your report never reach us.
  • This site counts anonymous visits with Google Analytics. Nothing you type is included.
  • AI advice can be wrong or out of date. Open the sources and check important facts before you act.
  • This is general guidance, not legal, tax, financial or investment advice. Talk to a qualified professional before major decisions.
